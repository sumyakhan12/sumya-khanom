## Brand & Style

The design system projects clinical authority, digital efficiency, and deep civic trust. Engineered specifically for patients, clinicians, and hospital administrators in Bangladesh, the interface bridges modern healthcare technology with intuitive accessibility across diverse digital literacies.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Corporate** structure with **Soft Tonal** warmth. By pairing an intentional clinical deep teal with soothing mint washes and soft slate tones, the system removes the cold sterility of traditional clinical portals without sacrificing procedural seriousness. Layouts maintain high information density while safeguarding generous negative space around critical clinical workflows (triage states, appointment timings, doctor credentialing, and prescription summaries).

Key design principles:
- **Calm Authority**: Visuals must reduce physiological tension for patients scheduling care or awaiting test results.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium**: Layouts gracefully support dynamic length variance between English and Bangla (বাংলা), accounting for taller ascenders, descenders, and differing line volumes.
- **Frictionless Navigation**: High-contrast focal cues guide users through multi-step scheduling, BDT payment flows, and doctor discovery.

## Colors

The color palette centers on clinical precision, biological wellness, and unambiguous semantic feedback. The default color mode is strictly `light`, establishing clean daylight legibility and clinical cleanliness across handheld devices in bright outdoor and fluorescent hospital settings.

- **Primary Deep Teal (`#087F75`)**: The core anchor of the system. Conveys surgical confidence and institutional stability. Reserved for primary actions, selected navigation states, major brand markers, and key interactive highlights.
- **Secondary Soft Mint (`#E7F5F1`)**: The environmental balance. Used for active card backdrops, chip backgrounds, calendar date highlights, and subtle section headers. Provides soft contrast against deep teal without eye fatigue.
- **Tertiary & Feedback Green (`#10B981`)**: Semantic validation for confirmed appointments, verified doctor credentials, available slots, and successful digital transactions.
- **Neutral Deep Navy (`#172B3A`)**: Replaces generic pure black to offer softer, higher-end contrast for all primary body and heading copy.
- **Muted Slate (`#718096`)**: Secondary and tertiary text, supporting metadata, helper captions, doctor BMDC license numbers, and past visit timestamps.
- **Border Teal-Grey (`#E2E8F0`)**: Low-friction structural lines that organize dense clinical information without cluttering viewports.
- **Background Surfaces**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, forms, and overlays; Light Neutral (`#F7FAFA`) for the canvas background to minimize glare.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Manrope** across all typographic tiers. Its geometric clarity, wide apexes, and generous counter shapes offer superior legibility on high-DPI displays as well as low-spec mobile panels common throughout regional hospital environments.

### Bilingual Handling (English & Bangla)
Bangla script requires an increased vertical bounding box compared to Latin characters due to matras (overbars) and lower vowel signs. When rendering Bangla strings:
- Increase standard `lineHeight` values by at least 15% to 20% to prevent diacritic clipping.
- Use explicit font fallbacks (`"Manrope", "Hind Siliguri", "Noto Sans Bengali", sans-serif`) to maintain optical weight parity between language toggles.
- Never set Bangla in uppercase or synthetic italics.

### Numeric & Currency Formatting
- Consultation fees, bed charges, and discounts must pair the official Bangladeshi Taka symbol (`৳`) directly with tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align prices uniformly across booking summaries and checkout lines (e.g., `৳ ১,২০০` or `৳ 1,200`).

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column 4-grid unit flow on mobile handhelds.

### Layout Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, max-width 1280px centered container, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Ideal for split clinician dashboards, dual-column schedule views, and patient health history tables.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column grid, dynamic fluid margins (`1.5rem`), allowing side-panel collapse into sliding drawers.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column architecture, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins. Doctor cards and appointment slots reorient to full-width horizontal rows for thumb-driven interaction.

### Vertical Rhythm
A base 8px system governs all element spacing. Compact metadata clusters (doctor specialty tags, schedule chips) utilize 4px (`space-xs`) or 8px (`space-sm`) gaps, while section partitions enforce 24px (`space-lg`) to 32px (`space-xl`) offsets to prevent visual overload during critical decision-making.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is delivered via **ambient, tinted shadows** paired with deliberate **tonal surfaces**, bypassing harsh drop-shadows to ensure the interface feels airy, welcoming, and clinically precise.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Light neutral surface (`#F7FAFA`). No elevation. Base substrate for all page routes.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.03);`
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Active Selection, Dropdowns)**: Elevated cards, active doctor selection panels, and quick-filter dropdowns:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 20px -3px rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sheet Drawers)**: Critical triage alerts, payment confirmation dialogs, and mobile bottom booking sheets:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -6px rgba(23, 43, 58, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -4px rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.06);`

All floating elevated elements must retain a thin boundary trace (`#E2E8F0`) to ensure contrast separation on low-tier screens with varied color calibration.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a generous **rounded-2xl** geometry for primary content containers, establishing approachable softness while maintaining clear functional zones.

- **Primary Cards & Containers**: Use `rounded-2xl` (16px / `1rem`) corner radii. This geometry encloses doctor profile cards, clinic location overviews, and appointment booking modules.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, & Dropdowns**: Use `rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`) for crisp geometric precision and distinct tap boundaries.
- **Status Badges, Filter Chips, & Timestamps**: Use pill geometry (`9999px`) to create organic contrast against structural rectangular cards.
- **Modal Dialogs & Mobile Bottom Sheets**: Top edges of mobile slide-over sheets feature a specialized 24px (`1.5rem`) radius to communicate touch-friendly draggable mechanics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Teal (`#087F75`) fill, pure white text, 8px border radius, font weight 600. Hover shifts to `#06655D` with subtle Level 2 shadow. Focus ring: 3px solid `#E7F5F1`.
- **Secondary**: Mint surface (`#E7F5F1`), Deep Teal (`#087F75`) text. No border. On hover, background shifts to `#D6ECE6`.
- **Outline**: Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#087F75`, text `#087F75`.
- **Destructive/Emergency**: Red fill (`#EF4444`) or light red tint background (`#FEF2F2`) with red text for cancelling active appointments.

### Medical Badges & Chips
- **Verification Badges**: Pill-shaped container, `#E7F5F1` background, `#087F75` text, featuring a verified medical tick icon.
- **Availability Chips**: Interactive time-slot selectors. Default: White background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Selected: Deep Teal background, white text, 0 shadow. Disabled/Booked: Light grey surface (`#EDF2F7`), muted slate text with strike-through or low opacity (0.5).

### Input Fields
- Height: 48px to allow easy finger tapping on mobile devices.
- Inactive state: Pure white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, Navy text (`#172B3A`), Muted Slate placeholder (`#718096`).
- Active/Focused: 1.5px solid `#087F75` with an ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px #E7F5F1`).
- Error state: 1.5px solid `#EF4444` accompanied by red contextual help text below the field.
- Mobile phone fields must feature a fixed prefix badge displaying the Bangladesh flag and country code (`+880`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px size with 6px border radius (checkbox) or circular form (radio).
- Unchecked: `#E2E8F0` stroke, pure white interior.
- Checked: Deep Teal (`#087F75`) fill with a crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Healthcare Cards (Doctor & Appointment)
- Structure: Pure white surface, 16px radius, Level 1 shadow, 1px `#E2E8F0` outer edge.
- Layout: Left-aligned avatar (with online/offline indicator dot), doctor details stacked vertically (Name, Degrees, Specialty, BMDC registration number in slate caption), horizontal divider, footer containing fee representation in BDT (`৳ ১,০০০`) and primary "Book Appointment" button.

### Localized Price & Payment Modules
- BDT currency symbol (`৳`) rendered in semibold weight, slightly smaller than the numeral (e.g., 14px symbol next to 18px price string).
- Native integration tags for local payment gateways (bKash, Nagad, Rocket) highlighted using soft neutral-bordered badges with standard height constraints (32px).
