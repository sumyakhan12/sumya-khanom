## Brand & Style
The design system fuses editorial precision with digital bio-tech vitality. It delivers an interface that feels scientifically rigorous yet emotionally uplifting, ditching sterile clinical blues in favor of deep organic teals, revitalizing mints, and an energetic lime accent.

- **Atmosphere:** Modern, calm, hyper-organized, and breathing with generous whitespace.
- **Design Movement:** Clean Modern Editorial with Subtle Organic Softness. Structured typography pairs with airy canvas spaces, pill-form tokens, and crisp high-contrast interactive elements.
- **Target Audience:** Patients, practitioners, and clinical researchers who value modern, approachable, and frictionless healthcare orchestration.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires deep institutional trust, clinical transparency, and a buoyant sense of human well-being.

## Colors
The palette leverages a crisp, warm off-white canvas paired with deep clinical teal for authority and fresh mint/lime tints for optimism and engagement.

- **Primary (`#087F75`):** Deep Teal. Used for primary CTAs, critical headers, prominent active states, and focal brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#DDF5EC`):** Fresh Mint. Serves as soft container fills, card highlights, and calm foundational blocks.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#C7F36B`):** Fresh Lime. High-energy indicator used sparingly for vital status badges, micro-interactions, conversion triggers, and progress metrics.
- **Neutral Core (`#172B2A`):** Deep Charcoal. Replaces pure black to ground typography, structural iconography, and borders with organic softness.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#FAFCFA` (Warm Off-White)
  - Card & Module Surfaces: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle Tonal Wells: `#F0F7F4`
  - Soft Aqua Shading: `#BFEDE5`
  - Subtle Borders & Dividers: `#EBF3F0`
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success / Available: `#10B981` (with `#E8F8F2` surface)
  - Live / Attention Indicator: `#F59E0B`
  - Muted / Inactive Slate: `#94A3B8` (Border/Stroke: `#E2E8F0`)

## Typography
Manrope acts as the singular typographic anchor, utilizing its geometric construct and open aperture to balance clinical legibility with warm editorial punch.

- **Headlines & Display:** Tightly tracked with generous line-heights. Bold weights (`700`, `800`) provide an authoritative, editorial medical publication feel.
- **Body Text:** Uses natural regular weights (`400`) at comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to maintain effortless parsing across complex diagnostics, notes, and patient records.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Slightly elevated letter-spacing (`0.02em`–`0.04em`) with medium-to-bold weights to optimize glanceability inside pill tags, status bars, and table cells.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model follows a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to prioritize content breathing room.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) outer margins with a maximum container bounding box of 1360px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer canvas margins. Multi-column metric sets fold into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (&lt;768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer canvas margins. Data tables convert to card stacks, and horizontal filter lists scroll freely along the x-axis.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Layout elements use multiples of 8px. Spacing between disconnected modules scales aggressively up to `space-xl` (40px) to prevent cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through tonal layering and ambient soft shadows rather than harsh physical skeuomorphism or stark dark mode contrasts.

- **Surface Layering:** The foundational floor is `#FAFCFA`. Surface panels (`#FFFFFF`) rest on top with hairline perimeter strokes (`1px solid #EBF3F0`). Secondary containers (`#F0F7F4` or `#DDF5EC`) provide distinct recessed grouping without requiring borders.
- **Ambient Shadows:**
  - *Resting Cards / Inputs:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 43, 42, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Elements & Dropdowns:* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(23, 43, 42, 0.04)`
  - *Active Modals:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(23, 43, 42, 0.12)` accompanied by a soft teal tinted backdrop overlay (`rgba(23, 43, 42, 0.25)`).
- **Edge Definition:** Soft borders (`#EBF3F0`) accompany elevated cards to preserve crisp definition on high-DPI displays.

## Shapes
A balanced rounded shape scale (`roundedness: 2`) establishes warmth and digital ergonomics while maintaining professional stability.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, utility buttons, contextual menus, tooltips, and data cells.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Applied to primary content cards, analytics modules, modal frames, and patient summary panels.
- **Extra Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for hero callouts, promo banners, and major dashboard segments.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Strictly deployed for status badges, tags, category pills, avatar clusters, and quick-filter button groups.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Deep teal (`#087F75`) fill, white text, 8px radius, medium weight (`600`). Hover elevates to `#06675F` with subtle ambient shadow.
  - *Accent:* Fresh lime (`#C7F36B`) fill with deep charcoal text (`#172B2A`) for high-priority actions and appointment booking CTAs.
  - *Secondary:* Soft mint (`#DDF5EC`) fill, deep teal text (`#087F75`), no border.
  - *Tertiary / Ghost:* Transparent background, deep charcoal text, borderless with deep teal hover state.
  - *Sizing:* 40px standard touch height, 48px for hero CTAs; 16px horizontal internal padding.

- **Pills & Status Chips:**
  - Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), 24px–28px height, 10px horizontal padding, `label-sm` font.
  - *Active / Available:* `#DDF5EC` surface, `#087F75` text, accompanied by an optional 6px `#10B981` pulsing dot.
  - *Live Alert / Caution:* `#FEF3C7` surface with `#B45309` text.
  - *Neutral / Inactive:* `#F0F7F4` surface with `#94A3B8` text.

- **Cards & Containers:**
  - Surface: pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px radius (`rounded-lg`), padded with 24px (`space-lg`), and framed by a 1px border (`#EBF3F0`).
  - Optional header zone uses a `#F0F7F4` tonal top section to cleanly separate metadata from clinical actions.

- **Input Fields & Selectors:**
  - Height: 44px, white background, 8px radius, border `1px solid #EBF3F0`.
  - Placeholder: `#94A3B8`; Text: `#172B2A`.
  - Focus state: border color smoothly shifts to `#087F75` accompanied by a 3px ring of `rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.15)`.

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Checkboxes use 6px rounded squares; Radios are circular.
  - Unchecked: 1.5px border `#E2E8F0`, white background.
  - Checked: `#087F75` background with a crisp white checkmark or center pip.

- **Lists & Data Tables:**
  - Alternating rows avoid heavy zebra patterns; instead, employ an ultra-subtle border-bottom (`#EBF3F0`) with a gentle hover fill (`#F0F7F4`).
  - Table headers use uppercase `label-sm` typography in muted slate (`#94A3B8`).

- **Domain-Specific Components:**
  - *Vitals Metric Card:* Prominent headline-md number paired with a compact sparkline rendered in `#087F75` and an accent delta tag in `#C7F36B`.
  - *Provider Availability Bar:* Segmented horizontal calendar bar showing open appointments marked in `#10B981` and unavailable slots in `#E2E8F0`.
